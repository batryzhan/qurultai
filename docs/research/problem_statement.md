# Research problem

Can structured multi-agent debate improve correctness, citation support, and confidence calibration compared with one answer and independent majority voting, at what latency and resource cost?

Qurultai targets students and researchers who need to inspect competing answers and their evidence. Agreement is observable behavior; correctness requires an external reference. The three biys are symbolic reasoning roles, not historical impersonations or proof of model independence.

The initial deliverable is an inspectable prototype and reproducible evaluation workflow. No local empirical superiority result has been established. Implementation status remains provisional until the integration checks in `../IMPLEMENTATION.md` are recorded.
